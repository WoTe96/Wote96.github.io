Who I Am

About Me

IT Project Manager, Certified ScrumMaster®, and technical communicator with 5+ years delivering enterprise technology initiatives at UCF.

I'm an IT Project Manager I at the University of Central Florida, embedded within the UCF IT Project Management Office. I bring 5+ years of experience leading technology initiatives, managing full project lifecycles, and coordinating cross-functional teams to deliver scalable, impactful solutions.

Currently serving as Scrum Master for UCF IT's AI/Automations team — facilitating two-week sprint cycles, agile ceremonies, and building replicable frameworks for other teams to adopt. I also design and lead AI-focused workshops and knowledge transfer sessions to help staff leverage emerging technologies responsibly and effectively.

I hold a Master of Arts in Technical Communications from UCF with a focus on Project Management and Technical Writing, complemented by a Certified ScrumMaster® credential and an active pursuit of the PMP certification.
